About the show

Hard Racing is for people who love racing on the edge: speedway bikes sliding sideways round a shale oval with a single gear and no brakes. Each episode brings race reviews, rider talk and a look at what is coming up. When the story is bigger than speedway, we follow it onto the MotoGP grid and the Superbike paddock.

Speedway

Our home track. League racing, Grand Prix, the riders and the rivalries.

MotoGP

The sport's top class, covered when it throws up a story worth the airtime.

Superbikes

Production-based machines and close racing, from the paddock to the podium.
